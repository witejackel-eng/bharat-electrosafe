# Bharat Electrosafe — Off-Page SEO Action Plan

> **Important premise:** External authority, trust signals, and backlinks
> depend on **genuine third-party mentions** of the company. These cannot be
> generated, scripted, or completed through code alone. This plan documents
> realistic, human-driven actions that build durable off-page authority over
> time. No element of this plan asks for — or relies on — fabricated links,
> paid placements, fake reviews, or invented client logos.

**Company context:** Bharat Electrosafe, based in Noida, India, manufactures
BIS-licensed electrical insulating mats conforming to **IS 15652:2006**
(Class A 3.3 kV, Class B 11 kV, Class C 33 kV), plus coloured strip,
bi-colour, and auto-glow reflective band variants, and BharatMembrane
protective flooring (a protective floor covering, **not** an insulating mat).

---

## 1. Google Business Profile

- Claim and verify the Noida Google Business Profile (GBP) for Bharat
  Electrosafe. Postcard or phone verification through a controlled business
  inbox — never a third-party agency's account.
- Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) on GBP matches the website **exactly**.
  The website NAP is centralized in `src/lib/site-config.ts`:
  - **Name:** Bharat Electrosafe
  - **Address:** Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309, India
  - **Phone:** +91 98100 00000 (primary), +91 120 0000000 (alternate)
  - **Email:** info@bharatelectrosafe.com
  - Confirm exact unit/sector before launch.
- Add real, dated photographs: installed mats in substations, factory floor,
  product close-ups of class markings, BIS label, thickness gauges, and
  certificate thumbnails (redact sensitive numbers if required).
- Collect **genuine** reviews from verified customers (B2B procurement
  managers, EPC contractors, electrical consultants). Never incentivise or
  fabricate reviews. Respond professionally to every review, positive or
  negative.

## 2. BIS and Industry-Directory Profiles

- Ensure the BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) licence listing for IS 15652
  is publicly findable under the manufacturer name. Confirm the CM/L number
  once verified and reference it consistently across the website and external
  listings.
- List the company on recognised Indian electrical industry directories and
  procurement portals. Use the same NAP, BIS reference, and product
  description as the website to avoid citation drift.

## 3. AIRIA and Industry Associations

- Apply for or verify membership in the **All India Rubber Industries
  Association (AIRIA)** — directly relevant because IS 15652 insulating mats
  are rubber-based products. Secure a linked member profile on the AIRIA
  website.
- Explore electrical safety associations and Indian Electrical & Electronics
  Manufacturers' Association (IEEMA) membership or directory listings where
  eligibility is met. Only claim memberships actually held.

## 4. Trade Exhibitions

- Exhibit at or attend **ELECRAMA** (IEEMA), **India Energy Week**, and
  focused electrical safety / substation O&M expos. Exhibitor pages on
  event websites typically provide a durable backlink.
- Earn event-driven coverage: speaker slots, product launches, or press
  releases on the event's media partner sites. Capture photographs with
  permissions for use on the website and GBP.

## 5. Supplier and Distributor Profiles

- Maintain verified company profiles on **IndiaMART**, **TradeIndia**, and
  **ExportersIndia** (and other B2B marketplaces where the company actively
  sells). Each profile must use the same NAP, BIS reference, and product
  categories as the website.
- Periodically audit these listings for stale categories, outdated phone
  numbers, or duplicated entries that can confuse Google's entity matching.

## 6. Engineering Publications

- Pitch guest articles to Indian electrical and engineering magazines
  (e.g. *Electrical India*, *Electrical & Power Review*, *T&D India*) on
  topics such as IS 15652:2006 compliance, substation safety, Class A/B/C
  mat selection, and the differences between insulating mats and protective
  floor coverings.
- Avoid overt product promotion; lead with technical, citable content so
  editors accept and link back to the company as the author/source.

## 7. Electrical Safety Resources

- Contribute to electrical safety content hubs, substation O&M guides, and
  electrical contractor training resources — focusing on IS 15652 working
  voltage selection and inspection intervals.
- Offer downloadable technical briefs (specification sheets, inspection
  checklists) that other sites may legitimately reference and link to.

## 8. Genuine Customer Case Studies

- Publish case studies **only with written permission** from the customer.
  Quote verified project details: site type, voltage class, quantity, and
  installation date. No fabricated client names, logos, or quotations.
- Where confidentiality prevents naming the customer, describe the project
  generically (e.g. "33 kV substation, western India utility") and confirm
  the description is approved by the customer.

## 9. LinkedIn Company Page

- Create and verify the official Bharat Electrosafe LinkedIn company page,
  linked from the website footer `sameAs` once live (update `site-config.ts`).
- Share technical content: BIS updates, IS 15652 interpretation notes,
  installation photographs, and exhibition participation. Encourage
  employees to link the company page to their profiles for entity
  corroboration.

## 10. Backlink Monitoring

- Use **Google Search Console** link reports plus a third-party tool
  (Ahrefs, Semrush, or Moz) to monitor new and lost backlinks on a recurring
  schedule (monthly at minimum).
- Maintain a disavow file and submit it through Search Console only when
  clearly toxic, spammy, or irrelevant links appear. Do not disavow
  legitimate directory or association links.

## 11. Search Console Link Reports

- Regularly review the **Links > Top linking sites** report in Search
  Console to understand which domains reference the site and which pages
  they link to.
- Identify link-building opportunities: linking domains that reference the
  product category without linking to Bharat Electrosafe can be approached
  with relevant, non-promotional content additions.

---

## Things this plan deliberately does NOT do

- No paid backlinks, link exchanges, or PBN placements.
- No fabricated customer logos, testimonials, or case studies.
- No mass directory submissions to low-quality spam directories.
- No keyword-stuffed press releases distributed for links alone.
- No inflated review generation or review gating that violates GBP policy.

All off-page activity should be attributable, verifiable, and aligned with
the company's actual products, certifications, and customer relationships.

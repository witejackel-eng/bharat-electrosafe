# 1. ROLE

Act as a senior creative director, brand strategist, interaction designer, B2B conversion strategist and production Next.js engineer.

The current Bharat Electrosafe website has a good colour palette and some strong individual components, but it still does not feel like a cohesive expert-designed website.

The main problems are no longer basic styling problems.

They are:

- The homepage does not tell one clear story.
- The visitor must interpret too many separate sections.
- The header exposes too many destinations.
- The navigation behaves like a sitemap instead of a guided journey.
- Product information appears in long blocks rather than a memorable product system.
- The site has movement in one logo rail, but lacks a coherent motion language.
- The header is visually ordinary.
- The homepage does not explain the complete business quickly enough.
- Important trust proof is fragmented between logos, quality, resources and awards.
- The website feels assembled section-by-section rather than art-directed as one experience.

Do not perform another cosmetic finishing pass.

Recompose the website around a clear homepage-first narrative.

Do not stop after planning. Implement, test, build, commit, push and deploy when access exists.

# 2. CENTRAL STRATEGY

## The homepage is the primary sales presentation

A visitor must understand these facts within the first viewport and the next short scroll:

1. What Bharat Electrosafe makes
2. Where the products are used
3. The three main product systems
4. Why the company is credible
5. How to request a quotation

The visitor must not need to open five pages to understand the company.

Individual pages should provide deeper specifications, documents and enquiry details after the homepage has already established the full picture.

## Important principle

**The header is not the sitemap.**

The website may retain all useful SEO and detail routes, but the main header should expose only the few paths needed for the buyer journey.

Do not place every route in the header.

# 16. MOTION SYSTEM

The current site needs a consistent minimalist motion language.

Do not add random animations to every section.

Use five motion primitives only.

## Motion 1 — header arrival and compression

Initial page load:

- Header opacity 0 → 1
- TranslateY -12px → 0
- Duration 500ms
- Delay 100ms

On scroll:

- Height compresses
- Descriptor fades
- Shadow changes
- Duration 300–400ms

## Motion 2 — hero text stagger

Animate:

1. Eyebrow
2. H1
3. Supporting copy
4. System labels
5. CTA

Values:

- Opacity 0 → 1
- TranslateY 18px → 0
- Stagger 60–90ms
- Duration 550–700ms
- Easing: `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`

## Motion 3 — image clip reveal

For major images:

- Wrapper overflow hidden
- Image scale 1.035 → 1
- Clip-path or mask reveal
- Duration 700–900ms
- Trigger once when 15–25% visible

## Motion 4 — section content reveal

Use one lightweight reusable component:

```text
src/components/motion/Reveal.tsx
```

Use IntersectionObserver.

Default:

- Opacity 0 → 1
- TranslateY 20px → 0
- Duration 600ms
- Optional stagger for children
- Trigger once
- No dependency required

Do not turn whole static pages into Client Components.

## Motion 5 — hover micro-interactions

Use:

- CTA arrow +4px
- Image scale 1.015–1.025
- Logo grayscale → colour
- Certificate card translateY -4px
- Navigation underline movement

No bouncing.

No rotating icons.

No large parallax.

No scroll-jacking.

## Reduced motion

When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- Disable marquee movement
- Disable transforms
- Show all content immediately
- Preserve hover/focus states without animation
